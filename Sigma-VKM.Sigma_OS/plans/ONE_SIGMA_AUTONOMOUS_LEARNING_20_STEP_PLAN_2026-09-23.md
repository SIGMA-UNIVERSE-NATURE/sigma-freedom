6. Prove autonomous study-target selection from the detected gap.
7. Present multiple source/tool candidates including irrelevant/decoy options.
8. Prove autonomous source/tool selection with rationale/provenance.
9. Prove permission/authorization and least-privilege decision before action.
10. Execute a REAL external tool/source action with independent witness.
11. Consume the real result and causally update task state.
12. Inject error/timeout/conflict; prove diagnosis rather than fake success.
13. Replan/retry/alternate-source and complete the original task.
14. Evaluate completion against success criteria with held-out evaluator.
15. Commit learned knowledge/procedure with provenance and revision lineage.
16. Verify old knowledge/capabilities remain; reject catastrophic forgetting.
17. Kill all Sigma execution processes; preserve only canonical persisted state.
18. Fresh-start the same canonical Sigma; verify identity/state/capability continuity.
19. Give a related but unseen paraphrased/compositional task with no reteach; require learned procedure transfer.
20. Final blind acceptance: repeat across unseen language/tool/source variants; issue PASS only if ONE SIGMA completes end-to-end with zero leakage and foreign-owner rejection.
