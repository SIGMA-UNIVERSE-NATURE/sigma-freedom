- `TARGET_COUNTS_VALID=0`
- `TARGET_FIELDS_ENCODING_VALID=0`
- `TARGET_GAPS_VALID=0`
- `STATUS=TARGET_GAP_COUNT_ENCODING_INVALID`
- no state mutation.

The V1 post-VM oracle correctly marks `EXP_TCOUNTS=0` and `EXP_TARGET_FIELDS=0`, but its independent `EXP_TARGETS_VALID` derivation still validates target1 when an active candidate has target_count other than 1 or 2. This creates exactly one alignment failure and the dependent no-mutation/state-boundedness counter failures.
