Retention gates:
- every five-part learning-unit reference must be present and hash-valid;
- verification status must be VERIFIED;
- verifier must be marked independent;
- learner and verifier identities must differ;
- EVIDENCE_CLASS=UNVERIFIED_TOOL_OUTPUT is never retained;
- maximum 64 retained experience records;
- exact replay is idempotent;
- same EXPERIENCE_ID with different bytes is refused;
- malformed persistent state is refused without mutation;
- dirty prestate containing an unverified retained record is refused;
- dirty prestate containing duplicate EXPERIENCE_ID records is refused.

Important ownership:
DNA16A does not generate the hypothesis.
DNA16A does not choose the action.
DNA16A does not decide semantic truth.
DNA16A does not execute knowledge promotion.
Those inputs must ultimately come from admitted native SIGMA capabilities.

DNA16A is a persistent retention gate and learning-memory primitive, not the
complete autonomous-learning loop.
