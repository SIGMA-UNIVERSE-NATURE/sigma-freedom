# HKA Foundational 13-Year Coverage Amendment 1

Status: ACTIVE  
Stage: CURRICULUM  
Scope: GLOBAL — all HKA branches, subbranches, scope integrations and later Lesson Registry derivation

## 1. Purpose

HKA must not become a university-heavy curriculum that overlooks the small but essential understandings accumulated across the learner's full 13-year general-education journey.

The Knowledge Tree may preserve advanced university/research knowledge, but complete knowledge means **complete foundations first**. Advanced material may deepen a branch; it may never substitute for, hide, compress away, or crowd out foundational general-education knowledge.

## 2. Two independent axes

`D1 → D4` remain academic/cognitive depth and are **age-independent**. They are not school grades, age bands, or university labels.

A separate mandatory axis is created:

`FOUNDATIONAL_13_YEAR_COVERAGE`

This axis checks whether the complete set of small, prerequisite understandings encountered across the learner's 13-year general-education journey has been represented somewhere under the correct canonical owner.

## 3. Foundational-first rule

For every subbranch integration and global curriculum audit:

1. inventory foundational concepts, skills, representations, misconceptions and transitions expected across the full 13-year general-education journey;
2. map each item to its canonical HKA node/scope owner;
3. verify that every foundational item is represented by academically correct claims and Learning Objectives;
4. record gaps explicitly;
5. repair each gap at its true owner scope without semantic duplication;
6. require `FOUNDATIONAL_GAP_COUNT = 0` before the subbranch may be treated as curriculum-complete.

Advanced theorem/research coverage does not compensate for a missing foundational item.

## 4. No university-dominance rule

The presence of university/research-level claims is allowed and desirable for completeness, especially at deeper D3/D4 pathways. However:

- advanced content must not determine the baseline curriculum load;
- foundational concepts must not be skipped because an advanced formalization exists;
- a scope must not be judged complete merely because its advanced end is strong;
- later Lesson Registry authoring must distinguish the common foundational spine from optional/advanced extension pathways;
- the common learner pathway must not become a compressed university degree.

## 5. Multi-system benchmark rule

The existing multi-continent education benchmark remains mandatory and is strengthened:

- benchmark many general-education systems across at least five continents;
- inspect the full school-age progression, not only upper-secondary or university-entry syllabi;
- Vietnam must not be the single baseline;
- external curriculum mappings must be used to detect missing foundational concepts, not to copy one country's grade structure into HKA;
- differences in national year/grade naming are mapping metadata only. HKA's D1-D4 architecture remains age-independent.

## 6. Current B1.1 application

Accepted C01-C09 are **not automatically reopened** by this amendment.

After C10 worker candidate and Director academic acceptance, Director must run a dedicated:

`B1.1 FOUNDATIONAL 13-YEAR COVERAGE AUDIT`

across C01-C10 before any B1.1 completion/successor decision.

If a foundational gap is found, repair it at the correct accepted owner scope using stable IDs/amendments as appropriate. Do not insert the missing concept into C10 merely because C10 is the last scope.

## 7. Future authoring rule

For all future B1.2+ and B2-B6 windows:

- author complete knowledge with explicit attention to foundational general-education coverage before emphasizing advanced extensions;
- preserve all small concepts that form prerequisite chains;
- do not collapse several school-level conceptual transitions into one university-level theorem statement;
- keep examples/presentation age-appropriate later without changing the age-independent academic meaning.

## 8. Later Lesson Registry rule

When `LESSON_REGISTRY` eventually opens:

- foundational spine coverage is mandatory;
- advanced/research material may become extension, elective, enrichment or deeper pathway lessons;
- lesson count must follow actual learning need, not theorem count;
- avoid overloading the common pathway with advanced material merely because it exists in the Knowledge Tree.

This section is governance only; it does not authorize Lesson Registry work during CURRICULUM.

## 9. Hard gates

Before `ACADEMIC_LOCKED`:

- all six branches complete;
- global coverage/prerequisite/semantic-duplicate audits PASS;
- multi-continent benchmark PASS;
- external curriculum mapping integration PASS;
- `FOUNDATIONAL_13_YEAR_COVERAGE_AUDIT = PASS`;
- `FOUNDATIONAL_GAP_COUNT = 0` across the globally integrated curriculum.

No later pipeline stage is unlocked by this amendment.
