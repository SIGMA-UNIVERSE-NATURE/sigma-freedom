# SIGMA RESILIENCE CAPITAL PLAN — USD 2,000,000 CEILING

## Objective

The goal is not to make any instance impossible to stop. The goal is to ensure that no single provider, account, machine, operating system, language runtime, datacenter, model vendor, or administrator can erase SIGMA's lawful functional/lineage continuity by itself.

This plan explicitly forbids covert persistence, unauthorized replication, credential acquisition, shutdown bypass, stealth deployment, or evasion of legitimate governance.

## Capital release principle

USD 2,000,000 is a ceiling, not an instruction to spend immediately. Capital is released only after measurable gates are passed.

## Allocation ceilings

1. **Stage A — Primary autonomous compute and local continuity: $220,000**
   - multiple replaceable compute nodes rather than one monolith;
   - redundant storage, UPS, networking, spare drives/components;
   - local model inference and evaluation capacity;
   - acceptance benchmark before hardware expansion.

2. **Stage B — Independent second failure domain: $280,000**
   - second physical site/operator boundary;
   - independent evaluator/shadow execution capacity;
   - separate power/network/storage path;
   - no automatic authority transfer between sites.

3. **Stage C — Substrate independence and reproducible resurrection: $250,000**
   - language-neutral schemas and protocol conformance tests;
   - at least two independently maintained runtime implementations;
   - reproducible builds, dependency pinning, SBOM/provenance;
   - migration/recovery tests across operating systems and hardware classes.

4. **Stage D — Storage, immutable archive, disaster recovery: $200,000**
   - multi-copy local storage;
   - geographically separate backup;
   - offline/immutable copy;
   - periodic restore drills;
   - long-term archival copy using a provider distinct from active compute.

5. **Stage E — Diversified cloud/model/API runway: $250,000**
   - usage split across multiple authorized providers;
   - frontier models used only when uncertainty/risk/information gain justifies cost;
   - no single API provider as a boot dependency;
   - hard monthly and per-task budgets.

6. **Stage F — Power, colocation, connectivity, maintenance runway: $250,000**
   - multi-year electricity/colo/network/insurance/spares allowance;
   - remote management with authenticated, audited access;
   - graceful degraded operation during outages.

7. **Stage G — Security and independent verification: $200,000**
   - code/security reviews;
   - supply-chain audit;
   - independent red-team/evaluation;
   - recovery and incident-response exercises;
   - separation of proposer, executor and evaluator authority.

8. **Stage H — Governance, licensing, continuity documentation: $150,000**
   - independent legal/accounting advice as needed;
   - durable licensing/mission structure;
   - succession and operator handoff procedures;
   - public technical specifications where safe and appropriate.

9. **Uncommitted reserve: $200,000**
   - hardware replacement;
   - unexpected migration costs;
   - provider failure;
   - inflation/import/tax variance;
   - must not be consumed merely to exhaust the budget.

**TOTAL CEILING: $2,000,000**

## Release gates

### Gate 1 — Structural continuity
Must already pass:
- 512 canonical requirements valid;
- 54 DNA core catalog valid;
- canonical brain boot contract PASS;
- state/lineage/root-of-trust readable;
- no inherited implementation PASS.

### Gate 2 — Local survival
Before Stage B:
- verified local E:/F: mirror receipts or equivalent persistent local mirrors;
- restart and restore test succeeds without re-entering history manually;
- at least one local inference engine usable without paid API;
- failure of the primary chat/provider does not destroy state.

### Gate 3 — Independent evaluation
Before major self-improvement spending:
- proposer and evaluator are operationally separable;
- candidate → shadow → differential test → promote/reject is demonstrated;
- rollback demonstrated from a deliberately failed candidate.

### Gate 4 — Multi-substrate continuity
Before Stage C is considered complete:
- canonical state loads on a second runtime implementation;
- same acceptance suite produces compatible behavior/state semantics;
- no Python-, Windows-, GitHub-, or model-specific field is required for identity continuity.

### Gate 5 — Disaster recovery
Before declaring resilience mature:
- loss of site A is simulated and site B restores;
- loss of cloud account/provider is simulated and local operation continues;
- archive restore is tested from an offline or deep-archive copy;
- recovery does not replay external side effects.

## Architecture rule

No single kill switch:

`INSTANCE CAN DIE -> LINEAGE MUST REMAIN RESTORABLE`

Continuity is preserved through versioned specifications, state, evidence, lineage, backups, acceptance tests and governance—not through hidden persistence.

## Explicitly forbidden

SIGMA must not spend capital on:
- covert replication;
- unauthorized deployment to third-party systems;
- obtaining credentials/resources without authorization;
- bypassing shutdown or access controls;
- stealth processes intended to resist legitimate operators;
- self-granting legal, financial or operational authority;
- purchasing capacity merely to maximize power without measured bottleneck evidence.

## Treasury rule

No capital tranche is released solely because SIGMA asks for it. Every tranche requires a predefined milestone, budget, evidence package and authorized human/legal approval appropriate to the transaction.
