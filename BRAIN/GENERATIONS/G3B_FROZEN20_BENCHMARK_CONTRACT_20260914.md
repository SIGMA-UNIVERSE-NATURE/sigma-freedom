## Evaluation rule

A story is mechanically counted PASS by the frozen R13R14R2 evaluator only when the SIGMA-created summary artifact exists, is non-empty, and has at least 10,000 words. The frozen evaluator does not score semantic correctness; therefore `FROZEN_20_STORY=20/20` is necessary evidence for G3 promotion but is not sufficient by itself to prove narrative understanding.

G3B does not repair learners. Any learning/core change belongs to G3C/Master and must arrive as a separately admitted candidate with exact model/core/runtime identifiers.
