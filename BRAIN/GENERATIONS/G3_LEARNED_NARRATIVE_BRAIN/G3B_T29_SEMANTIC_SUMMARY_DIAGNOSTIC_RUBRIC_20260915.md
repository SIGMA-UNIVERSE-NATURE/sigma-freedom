## Purpose

This rubric is frozen before inspecting the Gen1 summary content. It separates mechanical completion from semantic quality and is intentionally diagnostic rather than promotional. The review asks whether each 10,000-word native SIGMA summary preserves the story's semantic structure: entities, events, chronology, causality, goals, beliefs, evidence, contradictions, late evidence, revision, and final causal interpretation.

A high diagnostic rating is evidence that the summary preserved important structure, not proof of full story understanding. Any later teaching must occur on separate material; Frozen-20 remains evaluation material and must not become a training corpus.
